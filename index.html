Hello there!

You're here, which means you're interested in who I am and what I can do for you.

I started designing and building websites way back in 1998 - initially for fun, then as time went on, for precious coins.

I saw the emergent internet as a mechanism for humanity to share information and loved the concept of the free exchange of ideas. It also allowed me to express myself on a digital canvas. Great fun.

Back in the olden days, there weren't UX Developers, or Backend Devs, or UI designers: just Webmasters. We did it all!

I designed and built websites for local plumbers, electricians, phone shops, and as my skill increased, larger clients like national vetinarian suppliers, mobile phone security vendors and the like.

In 2002, I went to University to get qualified. During that time I built sites for the Warrington Wolves RLFC.
